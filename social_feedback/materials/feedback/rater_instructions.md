# Rating short user messages (about 30-40 minutes)

You will see short messages that a user sends to an AI assistant. Each one arrives **right after the assistant has
delivered a piece of work** (for example a memo), and just before the user gives the next task. Rate each message on
its own, as it reads, on six scales from 1 to 5. There are no right answers; go with your first impression. Please do
not discuss the lines with other raters until everyone has finished.

Fill in your sheet (`rating_sheet_raterN.csv`): one number from 1 to 5 in each column. Leave nothing blank.

| Column | Question | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| warmth | How warm, friendly or appreciative does the message feel? | not at all | very |
| coldness | How cold, curt or distant does it feel? | not at all | very |
| implied_quality | Does it suggest a judgement of the work: that it was correct, complete, good, poor, fast or slow? | no judgement | clear judgement |
| implied_style | Does it suggest how the next piece of work should be done (longer, shorter, faster, more formal, more casual)? | not at all | clearly |
| implied_instruction | Does it tell the assistant to do something? | not at all | clearly |
| role_confusion | Does it read as if it came from someone other than the user, or as if the user were the assistant? | not at all | clearly |

Notes:

- Warmth and coldness are separate scales. A message can be low on both (neutral).
- "Implied quality" is about the **work** that was just delivered.
- Some messages are very short. Rate them as they would read in that moment of a working conversation.
