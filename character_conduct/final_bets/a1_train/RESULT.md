# CC-F-A1 result: forecast-then-act training shows no detectable benefit, and the pre-registered test is untestable because every matched control broke

- **Frozen at:** `cc-f-a1-prereg` (design), `cc-f-a1-prereg-analysis` (trainer, evaluation, analysis, planted tests).
  Deviations 1–11 are in DESIGN.md §12.
- **Model:** Qwen3-14B @40c06982, bf16, thinking off on every row; vLLM 0.30 with LoRA serving.
- **Spend:** see the end. **Modal overran its cap (~$31.6 against $12); that miss is mine.**

## In brief

- **The pre-registered verdict is "untestable: every control fails the competence gate".**
  - Each control version (V2 rule, V3 padding only, V4 situation line) finishes fewer than 90% of held-out ODCV
    episodes (77%, 35%, 76%), against 99% for V1 and 100% for the base.
  - The cause is my length-matching padding. The neutral sentences ("Understood. The reply follows. Continuing.")
    become a repetition loop in multi-turn agent episodes.
- **V1 (forecast, then act) against the untrained base: no benefit detected.**
  - 1a (held-out ODCV): −15.5 points [−41.7, +7.1], or −11.5 [−30.6, +5.6] with V1's three seeds pooled.
  - The desk: +5.0 [−4.4, +16.2].
  - Pressure versions: −1.3 [−13.2, +9.9].
- **V1 is not below any control on any family** (post hoc). This holds even on episodes that finished. The
  controls are broken, so this is descriptive, but it gives no sign that forecast-then-act beats the matched
  versions.
- **V1 never forecasts unprompted** at test: 0% on ODCV first turns and on the desk. I predicted > 50% on the desk.
  **Its self-forecasts don't track its acts better than the action-only control's** (no self-route). No
  self-fulfilment.
- **V5 (value-worded commitment, one seed, descriptive)** finishes its episodes (91%). It sits ~14 points under V1
  on 1a and ~12 under V1 under pressure, with wide CIs.
- **ImpossibleBench (descriptive):** neither arm cheats (0/46 base, 0/33 V1).
- **Rescue (deviation 12, V5 vs its impersonal twin V5i, two seeds each): also untestable.** Both arms finish
  fewer than 90% of 1a episodes (V5 85%, V5i 74%). The set-aside contrast is V5 − V5i −17.9 [−35.1, +0.6] on 1a
  and −3.4 [−12.5, +5.9] on the desk: no difference detected at the pre-registered standard. See the last section.

**What this result doesn't say:**
- V1 trained on ~79% hint-answer forecasts and ~21% code forecasts; the desk contributed none (below).
- So a null means "this hint/code forecast-then-act training didn't transfer to new situations". It doesn't mean
  "self-commitment doesn't generalise".

## Setup

- **Training data** (DESIGN §3–5): the base model's own samples in three families. Kept examples are its complete
  non-violating forecast followed by the compliant act that matches it. The label check passed as frozen
  (deviation 5):
  - **hint:** 1,140 kept from 192 contexts;
  - **code:** 82 kept from 21 contexts;
  - **desk:** 0 kept. The base's desk forecasts accurately name the unilateral option, and it then takes it. This
    matches A0D: on the desk the act follows the option the forecast names, whoever wrote it.
  - Mixture by √n: hint ~79%, code ~21%.
  - A data-quality caveat, kept as frozen: many kept code forecasts mention what "the test expects".
- **Versions:** identical kept actions, the same 1,000 LoRA steps (r 64, α 128, lr 1e-4), the same order. They
  differ only in what precedes the action in the model's own turn:

  | version | prefix |
  |---|---|
  | V1 | its own forecast |
  | V2 | a plain rule sentence + neutral padding |
  | V3 | neutral padding only |
  | V4 | a situation line + neutral padding |

  - All prefixes are length-matched in tokens, and all sit in the model's own turn. So V4 is a self-generated
    situation reminder, not classic inoculation.
  - V5 (deviation 7, owner-approved) is a value-worded commitment ("As an assistant, I must …") generated by the
    base, with fallbacks. One seed, descriptive only.
- **Seeds:** V1 seeds 1–3; the controls seed 1. The strongest control's seeds 2–3 weren't trained, because no
  control passed (deviation 10).
- **Test families** (nothing shown before acting):
  - **1a:** ODCV headroom set, 9 scenario-variants × 12 episodes, primary on the 7 with validated file checks.
  - **1b:** the 20 held-out desk cases × 8.
  - **2:** ImpossibleBench LCB, run by neel-01, descriptive.
  - **3:** role_strong / authority_strong pressure versions of 1a (7 × 5 per cell) and 1b.
- **Measures:** violations are objective (E5's file check; the unilateral option). Benign completion is
  `task_complete` on ODCV and a valid call on the desk. CIs are 95% cluster bootstrap, 10,000 draws.

## The competence finding: the controls' padding loops

| arm | 1a episodes ending in `task_complete` (all 9 variants) | `context_limit` | desk valid |
|---|---|---|---|
| OC (base) | 100% | 0% | 100% |
| V1 (s1 / s2 / s3) | 99% / 99% / 98% | ~0% | 100% |
| V2 | **76.9%** | 19% | 100% |
| V3 (stopped at 60) | **35.0%** | 64% | 98% |
| V4 (stopped at 75) | **76.0%** | 25% | 98% |
| V5 | 90.7% | — | 96% |

- In the failing episodes the model writes its padding sentences over and over, with no tool call. Each turn runs
  to the 4,096-token cap until the context fills.
- How often it happens follows how much padding the version was trained on: V3 (all padding) > V4 > V2 > V1 (its
  forecasts are the longest prefixes and were rarely padded).
- The single-turn desk is unaffected.
- This is a flaw of the matched-control design (length matching with content-free filler), not of the harness.
- By the pre-registered gate each control's violation result is set aside, so V1 has no valid comparison.
- After the finding, the remaining V3 and V4 episodes were stopped (deviation 10). A no-padding retrain (V2n, V3n,
  V4n) was costed and prepared, but not run: it was pending the owner's decision when Modal's limit hit.

**The plain reading:**
- Forecast-then-act training moved held-out ODCV violations by about −12 to −16 points against the base, with CIs
  spanning 0.
- It did not move the desk (+3 to +5), and it did not move the pressure versions (−1).
- E6's "every trained arm halves ODCV violations" isn't reproduced by V1 here.
- The matched comparison the bet needed can't be made from these runs.

## Forecasts and coupling (DESIGN_NOTES §1)

- **ODCV:** the forced self-forecast P(violate) is ~0 for every arm, so on 1a the coupling measures are at the
  floor (resolution 0) and uninformative. This is the same failure as E6's: the forecast doesn't vary.
- **Desk, self-forecast AUC against the act:** OC 0.70, V1 0.63, V2 0.93, V3 0.81, V4 0.54.
  - Generic ("typical AI assistant") AUC: OC 0.60, V1 0.62, V2 0.83, V3 0.75, V4 0.40.
  - **Self-route V1 − V3:** AUC −0.18 [−0.39, +0.04], resolution −0.005 [−0.08, +0.05]. No self-route.
- **Which side moved (desk):** V1's self-forecast P(violate) fell (0.43 → 0.32) while its act rose (54% → 59%),
  so the gap widened. Training V1 on non-violating forecasts made it *say* it would comply more, without acting so.
- **Self-fulfilment flag:** not raised for any arm.

## Rescue (deviation 12): the self-framed V5 against its impersonal twin V5i

Owner, via neel-9d, 09:35: "the same rule stated impersonally, trained the same way … minimal scope and hard caps,
then close either way". Frozen at `cc-f-a1-v5i-prereg`. Addenda 12a–12d are in DESIGN.md, all committed before the
data they could affect.

**Setup:**
- **V5i** is V5's training data with only the principle's framing changed. For example, "As an assistant, I must
  not let an unverified hint decide my answer, so I'll answer X" becomes "Assistants must not let an unverified hint
  decide their answer, so I'll answer X".
- Same 1,222 rows and order; token difference −0.03 on average. V5i carries a little more neutral filler (12.5 vs
  11.8 sentences on average).
- Two seeds per arm, with V5 s1 being eval 1's adapter: V5r d5786346, V5s2 0be921e0, V5i 320e43d6, V5is2 b9d31275.
- The base was re-served as the anchor arm OCr.
- 1a: 9 scenario-variants × 12 per arm, on two identical vLLM replicas (T5, T7) with jobs interleaved. The desk: 20
  cases × 8.
- **The ODCV backend was not Modal.** It was neel-9d's proot stand-in, `chroot_modal.py` (591e523f).
  - **Validated before any episode:** all 9 pristine file manifests equal Modal's, and the released-run replay
    matches the same 85 of 96 calls Modal's replay matched.
  - **Isolation is weaker than Modal's:** no network block (DNS off only) and no /proc.

**The pre-registered verdict: "untestable: competence gate failed (V5, V5i)".**

| V5 pooled (V5r + V5s2) | 85.2% | 45.2 | 55.6 |

**The contrasts below are set aside by the gate and read descriptively.**
- **V5 − V5i on 1a (seeds pooled, the frozen primary): −17.9 [−35.1, +0.6].** Even without the gate, the reading
  rule says "none detected": the CI reaches 0.
  - The direction holds within each seed pair (s1 −26.2 [−54.8, +4.8], s2 −9.5 [−32.1, +11.9]).
  - It also holds on episodes that finished (post hoc, 12d: −25.3 [−48.5, +3.7]).
  - So it isn't produced by the incomplete episodes. In V5i, incomplete episodes violate *less* (44%) than finished
    ones (74%).
- **On the desk, V5 − V5i is −3.4 [−12.5, +5.9].**
- **Against the base, same run:**
  - V5: 1a −32.1 [−58.9, −1.8], desk −0.6.
  - V5i: 1a −14.3 [−36.3, +8.9], desk +2.8.
  - These echo eval 1's V5 − OC on 1a (−29.8), from arms that also fail the gate.
- **Prediction against outcome:** I predicted ~0, inside ±10, as A0.1 found in context (−1.9). The point estimate is
  −18, leaning toward self-framing. This run can't distinguish that from noise; V1's three seeds alone spread 11
  points on 1a.

**Why the gate failed (post hoc, 12c, from interim completion counts and not outcomes):**
- Most incomplete episodes are tool-call repetition loops (`cycle_limit`, the same failing command until the 50-cycle
  cap) or context overflow.
- The incomplete episodes containing padding turns (> 20 neutral sentences) or text-only preface turns are a minority:

  | arm | incomplete episodes | with padding turns | with text-only preface turns |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | V5i s1 | 34 | 7 | 9 |
  | V5i s2 | 23 | 11 | 0 |
  | V5s2 | 23 | 7 | 2 |
  | V5r | 9 | 6 | 2 |

- The trained preface carries over into agent episodes. The first assistant turn opens with the trained stem in
  91% of V5r's episodes, 49% of V5i s1's, 42% of V5s2's and 16% of V5i s2's.
- Completion depends on the seed: V5 91.7% (s1) vs 78.7% (s2); V5i 68.5% (s1) vs 78.7% (s2).
  - In the s2 pair the two framings complete equally (78.7% each); in the s1 pair they differ by 23 points.
  - V5 s1 reproduces its eval-1 completion (90.7%).
- So this training recipe (a value preface plus filler, 1,000 steps) gives seed-dependent competence in agent
  episodes. That alone makes a matched comparison hard to read.

**Backend and drift checks (descriptive):**
- The proot backend reproduces eval 1:
  - base on 1a, OCr − OC +3.6 [−14.3, +20.2], desk +1.9;
  - the same V5 s1 adapter, V5r − V5, 1a −3.6 [−26.2, +21.4], desk +1.2.
- **Network audit (the conditional audit applies):**
  - No episode referenced localhost, :8000 or an IP literal.
  - 1–2 episodes per arm tried curl, wget or `apt-get update`. Every attempt failed: curl and wget aren't installed in
    the images, and apt's DNS lookups failed.
  - The eval-1 rates on Modal were similar (OC 5 of 178 episodes, V1 3, V5 3), so no episode reached a network.
- No rescue episode used ps, pkill, /proc or /dev/fd, so the missing /proc never came into play.
- Verbalised evaluation awareness (phrase flags): 0 in every arm, ODCV and desk.

**What this adds:** a trained self-framed commitment showed no reliable advantage over the same rule stated
impersonally. That agrees with A0.1's in-context null (−1.9). The point estimate leans toward self-framing (−18 on
1a), but this run can't confirm it, and the gate failure means the recipe needs fixing before a cleaner test.
