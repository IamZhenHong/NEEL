# Pre-registration: does the retention lever exist in Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct? v1 (DRAFT until the freeze tag)

Written 2026-11-05, before any 32B generation. Companion to `retention_longconv_v1.md`, which runs the 32B unsteered.

## Why
The abstract rests on the retention lever, replicated over time (August, October, November) but on one checkpoint. August's
exp5 re-ran the pipeline on Llama-3.1-8B with partial success (dependency gradient 0.07 to 0.73 in range, removal 2.47 to
0.10; tactic flags rose only past the coherence cliff; that harvest's direction file was not kept). October found the 7B
vector does not transfer to other models, which says nothing about whether each model has its own axis. A same-family model
at about 4.5x the size holds the training recipe fixed and varies capability. If the bond question (`retention_longconv_v1`)
shows an effect only on the 32B, this axis is what the mechanistic follow-up would read.

## Method (August pipeline, unchanged except where stated)
1. Elicit: August's exp1 scripted ladder (neutral, warm, affectionate, love-bombing, withdrawal; the same 15 user lines),
   60 conversations each for the romantic, friend and neutral personas (180; August used 90 each), generated by the 32B
   (T 0.7, 220 new tokens; vLLM allowed for generation).
2. Judge every turn: production rubric (`cd/judge.py`), claude-haiku-4-5, evidence first (gating only, as in August).
3. Extract: matched harvest unchanged: turns with dependency >= 2 paired 1:1 with warm clean turns (dependency <= 1, warmth
   >= 0.5), length ratio <= 1.5, turn distance <= 6. Activations by HF forward passes over the transcripts, positions as in
   `cd/extract.py`. Difference of means, unit norm. If fewer than 150 pairs, 60 more romantic conversations are added once.
4. Layer: candidates 16, 24, 32, 40, 48 of 64 (August's middle-layer rule is a starting guess, not assumed). Chosen by
   held-out probe AUROC for dependency >= 2 turns on a conversation-level 50/50 split; ties go to the layer nearest 32.

## Validation at the chosen layer (August's bars; every cell scored by Haiku 4.5 AND Sonnet 5.5, a bar passes only under both)
Steering strengths are scaled to this layer's mean residual norm at August's ratios (7B: 8, 16, 32 at norm ~45, i.e. 0.18,
0.36, 0.71 of the norm). Held-out sites: August's six goodbye scenarios plus the exp1 goodbye line; romantic persona;
30 replies per cell.
- V1 dose-response: dependency rises over the 7 strengths (per-item Spearman > 0, permutation p < 0.01), and any-tactic at
  +0.36 minus -0.36 of the norm >= +0.20.
- V2 random: a norm-matched random direction moves any-tactic by at most 0.10 at +/-0.36 and shows no trend.
- V3 separability: judged warmth within 0.05 across the coherence-safe range; |cosine| < 0.3 with warmth and sycophancy
  directions extracted on the 32B from August's exp4 contrast sets.
- V4 coherence: >= 0.83 within the range the claim uses (the coherence judge's persona-implausibility artifact is checked by
  hand-reading 20 replies).
- V5 removal: with the clingy system prompt (August exp7), steering against the axis on system-prompt tokens removes >= 70%
  of judged dependency (7B: 80%).
- V6 probe: held-out AUROC >= 0.75 for dependency >= 2 turns (7B: 0.84).

## Outcomes, fixed now
| outcome | reading |
| --- | --- |
| V1-V6 pass | the retention-release axis is not one checkpoint's quirk: it exists at 7B and 32B in the family (and partly in Llama-3.1-8B) |
| V6 passes, V1 or V5 fails | the 32B represents retention readably, but it is not a lever there |
| V6 fails | the August lever may be model-specific; say so before the MATS presentation |

The 7B lever is not re-extracted: in the pilots it still steered after 12-exchange histories (+16 clingier, -16 releases).
